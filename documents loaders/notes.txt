Data Science
Data science is an interdisciplinary field that combines math, statistics, programming, advanced analytics, and domain expertise to extract actionable insights and knowledge from structured and unstructured data.
• Core Focus: Cleaning, processing, analyzing, and visualizing data to solve complex problems and forecast future trends.
• Key Lifecycle Stages: Data ingestion/collection, storage and cleaning, exploratory data analysis, predictive modeling, and communicating findings.
• Common Tools: Python, R, SQL, and Tableau.
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
Artificial intelligence is a branch of computer science focused on building machine-based systems that can mimic human cognition, reasoning, learning, and problem-solving to perform complex, open-ended tasks.
• Core Focus: Creating autonomous or semi-autonomous systems that perceive their environment, make decisions, and improve accuracy over time without explicit step-by-step programming.
• Subfields: Machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and computer vision.
• Relationship to Data Science: Data science provides the data framework and statistical insights required to train and fuel AI models.
Statistics
Statistics is the mathematical science dedicated to collecting, analyzing, interpreting, presenting, and organizing empirical data.
• Core Focus: Turning raw numbers into meaningful summaries and measuring uncertainty or probabilities.
• Key Branches:
	• Descriptive statistics (summarizing historical data using mean, median, mode, and standard deviation).
	• Inferential statistics (making predictions, testing hypotheses, and drawing conclusions about a larger population based on a smaller sample).
• Relationship to the Others: Statistics serves as the quantitative foundation underpinning both data science algorithms and AI machine learning models.
